# Akbars Academy: Parent Guide

For parents and guardians. You use this app to see your child's attendance, fees, test results and notices, and to write to the academy.

**Open it:** https://attend.fiveminfotech.com (works on a phone or computer browser; nothing to install).

---

## 1. First sign-in

1. Open the link above.
2. Tap **Parent** (not Staff).
3. **Username:** the one the academy gave you. It looks like `P12012`.
4. **Password:** the temporary password the academy gave you.
5. Tap **Sign in**.
6. The app now asks you to **set your own password**. Type the temporary password once, then choose a new password that only you know. Next time, sign in with the new one.

**Forgot your password or username?** Ask the front desk. They can give you a new temporary password. There is no "forgot password" email.

---

## 2. The five tabs at the bottom

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Today at a glance: whether your child was marked present, and what's new. |
| **Calendar** | Attendance day by day, plus holidays. |
| **Alerts** | Messages the academy sent you, such as "arrived", "absent", or a notice. |
| **More** | Fees, test results, notice board, documents, leave, questions. |
| **Account** | Your details and change password. Sign out is here. |

If you have more than one child, switch between them on the Home screen.

---

## 3. What's under "More"

- **Fees:** what is due and every receipt you have been given.
- **Test results:** marks, rank and the class average.
- **Notice board:** holidays, meetings, functions.
- **Documents:** worksheets and notes the academy shares. Tap to open or download.
- **Leave:** apply for your child's leave (see below).
- **Questions and issues:** write to the academy and read their reply.

Some of these may not appear. The academy switches features on and off.

---

## 4. How attendance works

Your child presses a finger on the machine at the academy gate when they arrive and when they leave. That punch is recorded, and attendance appears in the app within a few seconds, usually with an alert to you.

- **Present / Absent:** shown on Home and in the Calendar.
- **Wrong or missing?** If your child did punch but the app says absent, write it under **Questions and issues** with the date. The front desk can correct it, and every correction is recorded with a reason.

---

## 5. Applying for leave

1. **More → Leave**.
2. Choose the date(s) and write the reason.
3. Send. You'll see the status (waiting, approved, or declined) in the same place.

Apply before the day when you can.

---

## 6. Asking a question

1. **More → Questions and issues**.
2. Start a new one, write your message, and send.
3. The academy's reply shows up in the same thread. You also get an alert.

---

## 7. Keeping your account safe

- Never share your password, not even with academy staff. Staff never need it.
- Sign out on shared phones or computers: **Account → Sign out**.
- Change your password any time under **Account**.

---

## Quick fixes

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| "Can't sign in" | Make sure **Parent** is selected, and check capital letters in the username. Still stuck? Ask the front desk for a new temporary password. |
| Page won't load | Check your internet, then refresh the page. |
| Attendance looks old | Pull down to refresh, or wait a minute and reopen. |
| Wrong child's data | Tell the front desk right away. |

**Help:** ask the academy's front desk, or write under **Questions and issues**.
